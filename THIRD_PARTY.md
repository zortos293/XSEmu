XCL is licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later** (`LICENSE`). Below is everything that comes from outside,
together with its license and how it is used. Files that contain logic ported from another project
carry a note in their header about its origin and the date of changes (GPL-3.0 §5a requirement).
